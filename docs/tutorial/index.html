Welcome to the Bits MFS middleware

A guided tour for a developer who has never seen this project, or a mobile money system, before. By the end you can explain what every service does, follow a payment from the phone to the ledger and back, run the whole system on your laptop and change one service safely.

The whole project in three sentences

Bits runs a mobile wallet: people keep money on their phone number and send it, pay with it, top up airtime and pay bills. The money itself (balances, the ledger) lives in a separate system called the core. This repository is the middleware: the layer in between that receives every request from apps, USSD phones, shops and portals, talks to the core and to outside partners in the right order, makes sure nothing is done twice or lost, and tells everyone the result.

How to read this tutorial

Read chapters 1 to 3 in order: they give you the vocabulary everything else uses. After that, chapters 4 to 7 each follow one family of features and can be read in any order. Do chapter 8 at your keyboard with the system running. Keep chapter 9 open when you write your first change. If a word is new, look it up in the glossary.

This is an MVP

The repository builds the first version (the "MVP"). Some services in the spec do not exist yet (mw-adapter-interop, mw-compliance, mw-recon), the real core is replaced by a mock and every partner is a simulator. The tutorial says so wherever it matters.
